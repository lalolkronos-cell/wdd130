History of Rafting

Rafting has its origins in the 1940s when explorers began navigating the wildest rivers in the world. Since then, this sport has evolved to become one of the most popular adventure activities.

Today, thousands of people enjoy rafting around the world, combining the excitement of navigation with the natural beauty of river landscapes. Our company has been guiding adventures since 1985.
