Rafting Trip Advisor

Our Purpose: To connect people with the raw beauty of nature through unforgettable river adventures.
Our Mission: To provide safe, exhilarating, and eco-conscious rafting experiences that foster a deep respect for the environment.
Our Motto: "Ride the Rapids, Preserve the Wild."

History

Founded in 2010 by a group of passionate river guides, Rafting Trip Advisor began as a small, local operation on the Colorado River. What started with two rafts and a dream quickly grew into a renowned adventure company, driven by a deep love for the water and a commitment to preserving the pristine wilderness. Over the years, we have expanded to offer trips on some of the most iconic rivers in North America, always prioritizing safety, environmental stewardship, and creating life-changing experiences for our guests. Our legacy is built on the laughter of thousands of adventurers and the unwavering belief that the best stories start with, "Remember that time on the river..."
